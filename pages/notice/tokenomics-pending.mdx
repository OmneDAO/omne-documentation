# Tokenomics finalization is pending

Specific economic parameters for Testum testnet and Primum mainnet — including reward schedules, fee multipliers, slashing ratios, and supply curves — are being finalized via formal multi-agent analysis (Economist + Mathematician + Research Analyst + Legal Strategy Analyst). Until that work is complete and reviewed, these values are not published.

This notice is referenced from tables in [architecture/economics](/architecture/economics) and [node-operations/genesis](/node-operations/genesis) where the unfinalized values would otherwise appear.

The Foundation does not publish placeholder economic parameters; partial values would mislead readers planning capacity, fee budgets, or governance positions. Once the formal analysis is reviewed and locked, this notice is replaced with the specific values + the rationale brief.
